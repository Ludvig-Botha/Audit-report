Boiler Water Level (report 6.1)
-------------------------------
Q1. Gauge glass / sight glass condition
    OK comment: Gauge glass is in a good condition.
    Finding:    Sight glass not clearly visible / leaking and needs replacement.
    Recommend:  Replace/repair sight glass. The gauge glass should be blown down once per shift to ensure it is working correctly and the standpipes do not become blocked with scale.
Q2. Gauge glass blown down each shift?
    OK comment: Gauge glasses are blown down each shift.
    Finding:    Gauge glasses are not blown down at the beginning of each shift.
    Recommend:  The gauge glasses should be blown down once per shift to ensure that they are working correctly and that the standpipes do not become blocked with scale.
Q3. Water level control type & condition
    OK comment: Water level control is in a good condition.
    Finding:    Water level Mobrey switches badly worn / water level control not ideal with slow response.
    Recommend:  Upgrade the water level control system by installing a Spirax LP21 probe, controller and a VSD for the feedwater pump for modulating control of the water level.
Q4. Extra low water cut-out condition & test
    OK comment: Extra low water cut-out was tested and is in a good condition.
    Finding:    Extra low water Mobrey worn / extra low water lock-out could not be verified.
    Recommend:  Upgrade the extra low water protection by installing a Spirax LP40 probe and extra low water controller. Blow down every shift to check that the boiler trips and locks out at extra low water level.
Q5. Level control blowdown pipework
    OK comment: Level control blowdown pipework is in a good condition.
    Finding:    Mobrey/level control blowdown pipework bent or damaged; blowdown not possible.
    Recommend:  Replace/repair the sight glass and level control blowdown pipework as soon as possible.
Q6. Low / extra low / high water alarms (audible + visual)
    OK comment: Low, extra low and high water alarms are working correctly.
    Finding:    Alarms for low and high water levels not correct: no audible low water alarm / no separate extra low alarm / no record kept of alarm tests.
    Recommend:  All alarms, audible and visual, should be recorded. If the water level in the gauge glass is correct and a low water alarm persists, the level controls should be checked; if the level remains low, check the feedwater pumps.
Q7. Records of alarm tests available?
    OK comment: Records of alarm tests are available.
    Finding:    Records of alarm tests could not be provided for review.
    Recommend:  Record all alarm tests in the daily logbook and retain records for review.
Q8. Feedwater pump condition & automation
    OK comment: Feedwater pump is in a good condition.
    Finding:    Feedwater pump faulty / cannot keep up with steam demand / no automation of feedwater pumps.
    Recommend:  Urgent attention should be paid to malfunctioning boiler feedwater pumps and to the automation controls of the feedwater pumps.
Q9. Feedwater pump protection installed?
    OK comment: Feedwater pump protection is installed.
    Finding:    No feedwater pump protection is installed on the pumps.
    Recommend:  Install feedwater pump protection to prevent pump damage.
Q10. Feedwater flow meter installed & working
    OK comment: Feedwater flow meter is installed and working.
    Finding:    No feedwater flow meter installed / feedwater meter not working.
    Recommend:  Install a feedwater flow meter (magnetic type recommended) for each boiler to monitor the amount of water used per boiler.
Q11. Cabling to level switches / transmitters secure
    OK comment: Cabling to level switches and transmitters is secure.
    Finding:    Cabling to feedwater pumps, level switches and/or transmitters is not secure and safe.
    Recommend:  Cabling to feedwater pumps, level switches and/or transmitters should be secured and made safe.
