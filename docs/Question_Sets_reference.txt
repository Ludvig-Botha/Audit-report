--- Boiler Water Level (report 6.1) ---

1. Gauge glass / sight glass condition   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Sight glass not clearly visible / leaking and needs replacement.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace/repair sight glass. The gauge glass should be blown down once per shift to ensure it is working correctly and the standpipes do not become blocked with scale.

2. Gauge glass blown down each shift?   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Gauge glasses are not blown down at the beginning of each shift.
   RECOMMENDATION: The gauge glasses should be blown down once per shift to ensure that they are working correctly and that the standpipes do not become blocked with scale.

3. Water level control type & condition   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Water level Mobrey switches badly worn / water level control not ideal with slow response.
   RECOMMENDATION: Upgrade the water level control system by installing a Spirax LP21 probe, controller and a VSD for the feedwater pump for modulating control of the water level.
   EQUIPMENT OPTIONS: Mobrey, Spirax LP20 probe, Spirax LP21 probe, K-Tek probe

4. Extra low water cut-out condition & test   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Extra low water Mobrey worn / extra low water lock-out could not be verified.
   RECOMMENDATION: Upgrade the extra low water protection by installing a Spirax LP40 probe and extra low water controller. Blow down every shift to check that the boiler trips and locks out at extra low water level.
   EQUIPMENT OPTIONS: Mobrey, Spirax LP30, Spirax LP40

5. Level control blowdown pipework   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Mobrey/level control blowdown pipework bent or damaged; blowdown not possible.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace/repair the sight glass and level control blowdown pipework as soon as possible.

6. Low / extra low / high water alarms (audible + visual)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Alarms for low and high water levels not correct: no audible low water alarm / no separate extra low alarm / no record kept of alarm tests.
   RECOMMENDATION: All alarms, audible and visual, should be recorded. If the water level in the gauge glass is correct and a low water alarm persists, the level controls should be checked; if the level remains low, check the feedwater pumps.

7. Records of alarm tests available?   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Records of alarm tests could not be provided for review.
   RECOMMENDATION: Record all alarm tests in the daily logbook and retain records for review.

8. Feedwater pump condition & automation   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Feedwater pump faulty / cannot keep up with steam demand / no automation of feedwater pumps.
   RECOMMENDATION: Urgent attention should be paid to malfunctioning boiler feedwater pumps and to the automation controls of the feedwater pumps.

9. Feedwater pump protection installed?   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: No feedwater pump protection is installed on the pumps.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install feedwater pump protection to prevent pump damage.

10. Feedwater flow meter installed & working   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: No feedwater flow meter installed / feedwater meter not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a feedwater flow meter (magnetic type recommended) for each boiler to monitor the amount of water used per boiler.

11. Cabling to level switches / transmitters secure   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Cabling to feedwater pumps, level switches and/or transmitters is not secure and safe.
   RECOMMENDATION: Cabling to feedwater pumps, level switches and/or transmitters should be secured and made safe.

--- TDS & Blowdown (report 5.2 per boiler) ---

12. Automatic TDS control installed & working   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Boiler does not have automatic TDS control / TDS system not working due to seized valves or faulty controller.
   RECOMMENDATION: Automatic TDS controls and water sample coolers need to be installed/upgraded on the boiler. TDS levels must be maintained between 2500-3000 to prevent water carry-over to the process.

13. TDS reading taken (record value)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: TDS measurement was extremely high, measured above specification with handheld tester.
   RECOMMENDATION: Maintain TDS between 2500-3000 by correct blowdown and dosing. Record TDS and pH for each shift in the daily logbook.

14. Sample cooler installed   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: No water sample cooler installed on the boiler.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a water sample cooler for safe and accurate boiler water sampling.

15. Blowdown valve condition & operation   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Boiler blowdown valve leaking / stiff / blowdown pipe blocked.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair/replace the blowdown valve. Blowdown valve flanges must be tight with effortless action.

--- Valves & Mountings ---

16. Safety valves condition (leaks / test records)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Steam leak on safety valves / safety valve test records not available.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace or overhaul safety valves with steam leaks. Safety valves to be tested with the AIA inspector and documented.

17. Main steam (crown) valve   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Steam leak on the crown valve.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace/repair the leaking crown valve as soon as possible.

18. Feedwater isolating & check valves   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Feedwater valve / check (one-way) valve passing or seized.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the feedwater isolating and check valves.

19. Boiler pressure gauge   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Boiler pressure gauge faulty / not calibrated.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the boiler pressure gauge with a newly calibrated pressure gauge.

20. Pressure control instrumentation & safety pipework   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Copper pipes used for boiler safety instruments; does not comply with BS EN 12953. High pressure lock-out drifting from set calibration.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace copper instrument pipework to comply with BS EN 12953. Replace/recalibrate the high pressure safety lock-out and incorporate the pressure transducer onto the new pipework.

21. Air release valve   [default priority: Low]
   FINDING: Air release valve leaking / seized.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair/replace the air release valve.

--- Boiler Mechanical & Maintenance (report 5.5) ---

22. Fire tubes condition & cleaning interval   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Fire tubes blocked / not cleaned regularly, reducing heat transfer.
   RECOMMENDATION: Fire tubes should be cleaned regularly, ideally every three to four months, to maintain optimum heat transfer and boiler efficiency.

23. Refractory condition   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Refractory is cracked/damaged which leads to loss of heat and efficiency.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair/recast the damaged refractory.

24. Smokebox doors, clamps & seals   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Smokebox door clamps/bolts missing; smokebox seals leaking.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace all missing smokebox door clamps/bolts and repair smokebox seals. Smokebox doors must be tight.

25. Exit flue gas temperature gauge   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Exit flue gas temperature gauge not working; monitoring of blocked tubes and combustion efficiency not possible.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the exit flue gas temperature gauge to monitor fire tube blockage and combustion efficiency.

26. Boiler instrumentation calibration   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Boiler instrumentation not calibrated.
   RECOMMENDATION: Calibrate or replace boiler instrumentation.

27. Control panel condition   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Control panel switches, buttons and pilot lights damaged or not working; wiring loose with risk of electrocution.
   RECOMMENDATION: Upgrade the panel with a new OEM automated control panel with HMI screen (and fan VSDs where applicable) compliant with pressure equipment regulations and EN boiler safety standards.

28. Safety interlocks tested   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Safety interlocks could not be verified / not tested.
   RECOMMENDATION: Yearly AIA safety inspections to be done: safety chain lockouts, low water alarm and lock-out, safety valves and E-stop to be tested and documented.

29. Statutory / AIA inspection records   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: The AIA hot commissioning report for the setting of safety limiters was not provided for review.
   RECOMMENDATION: Provide AIA inspection and hot commissioning records. The boiler must be subjected to internal and external inspection annually as required by law.

30. Maintenance schedules in place   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Weekly or monthly maintenance schedules could not be provided for review.
   RECOMMENDATION: Implement daily, weekly and monthly maintenance schedules for the boiler plant, steam distribution and condensate recovery equipment, and record all maintenance.

--- Combustion — Stoker (report 6.3) ---

31. Fuel analysis records available   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Fuel analysis records were not provided for review.
   RECOMMENDATION: Perform a proximate analysis, a mineral ash analysis and ash fusion temperatures at least once per coal delivery. Ensure the volatile content of fuel is within specification.

32. Combustion quality (red coals off stoker end?)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Incomplete combustion of fuel was occurring with red coals being discharged off the end of the stoker.
   RECOMMENDATION: Set up combustion controls, check undergrate air distribution and grate speed to ensure complete burnout before the end of the grate.

33. Boiler maintains pressure setpoint?   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: The boiler could not maintain the operating pressure setpoint.
   RECOMMENDATION: Investigate combustion controls, fuel quality and heating surface fouling; restore automatic control of ID and FD fans.

34. Stoker mat / links / link rods   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: The stoker mat shows signs of overheating with damaged links and bent link rods; ends of link rods damaged with drifting rods and missing split pins.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace damaged links and link rods and fit split pins. Use OEM parts only.

35. Grate tension   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: The stoker mat could not be tensioned due to excessive wear on drive links.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace worn drive links; remove a link from the stoker chain and jack the motor to correct tension.

36. Stoker level / frame condition   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: The stoker needs to be levelled in the boiler; the stoker frame shows signs of distortion.
   RECOMMENDATION: Level the stoker in the boiler and repair/replace the distorted frame sections.

37. Guillotine door & castings   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Guillotine doors not level; guillotine castings/refractory damaged; door height not displayed / encoder faulty.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair guillotine doors and castings; replace the guillotine door level indicator/encoder.

38. Ignition arch condition & seal   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Air leaks found on the ignition arch; ignition arch seal rope damaged.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace ignition arch seal rope and close air leaks to prevent cold air cooling the ignition arch.

39. Draught pipework & furnace pressure control (Photohelic)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Draught pipework to the ignition arch missing/damaged with no feedback to the Photohelic; no automatic control of ID and FD fans.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair draught pipework; replace the Photohelic with a Yokogawa pressure controller for automatic furnace pressure control.

40. Swinging chute installed & working   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: No swinging chute installed; air leaks from the coal hopper.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a swinging chute between the overhead fuel hopper and the stoker hopper. This also prevents air leaks at the coal hopper.

41. Coal hopper & shut-off doors   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: The stoker hoppers and shut-off doors need replacement; air leaks found on the coal hopper.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace stoker hoppers and shut-off doors; seal air leaks.

42. Undergrate dampers & actuators   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Undergrate damper actuators not installed / not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install/repair undergrate damper actuators for correct air distribution along the grate.

43. Stoker drive (motor, chain, V-belts, cover)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Stoker motor misaligned, chain loose and slipping, V-belts loose with slippage, no V-belt cover.
   RECOMMENDATION: Align the stoker motor, tension the chain, replace and tension V-belts and install a V-belt cover.

44. FD fan control   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: FD fan actuator missing / damper control poor.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a VSD to control the FD fan speed instead of damper and actuator for better control of air flow.

45. ID fan control   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: ID damper actuator not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a VSD for ID fan speed control. VSDs give finer, more accurate control than actuator-driven dampers.

46. O2 probe & analyser   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: No O2 probe/analyser installed; monitoring of efficient combustion is not possible.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a Lamtec LT2 O2 analyser per furnace and reduce flue gas oxygen content by adjusting the fuel trim and automating combustion control.

47. Temperature probes (ignition arch, rear roller, coal hopper)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Ignition arch / rear roller / coal hopper temperature probes not installed or broken.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install/replace the ignition arch, rear roller and coal hopper temperature probes.

48. Spy holes & air leaks   [default priority: Low]
   FINDING: Spy holes and air leaks around the stoker allow cold air in, cooling the ignition arch.
   RECOMMENDATION: Close spy holes and air leaks around the stoker to prevent cold air cooling the ignition arch.

49. Moisturising steam to coal   [default priority: Low]
   FINDING: No moisturising steam introduced into the coal in the stoker hopper.
   RECOMMENDATION: Introduce moisturising steam into the coal in the stoker hopper; this assists the fine fuel fraction to adhere and burn properly.

51. Grit collector DP gauges   [default priority: Medium]

   RECOMMENDATION: Install differential pressure gauges on the grit collectors and boilers to indicate the level of blockage.

52. Grit / ash trolleys & chute doors   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Grit trolleys, ash chute trolleys and ash chute doors damaged/leaking.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair and replace leaking/damaged ash chute doors, grit collector double flaps and trolleys.

53. Sootblowers   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Sootblowers not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair sootblowers and operate per the maintenance schedule.

54. Emission tests   [default priority: Low]
   FINDING: Emission test records not available.
   RECOMMENDATION: Emission tests to be completed.

--- Combustion — Burner (report 6.3 / 7.3) ---

62. Burner service records   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Records of the last burner service were not provided.
   RECOMMENDATION: Maintain records of burner services and work done. A bi-annual burner service is recommended to run burners at optimal efficiency.

63. Fuel/oil leaks at burner   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Leaking of HFO was observed at the burner / oil leaking into the cup on standby due to worn internal seals of the fuel block.
   RECOMMENDATION: Overhaul the burner fuel block with a full service kit and seal all oil leaks on burners and oil lines to prevent energy loss, safety hazards and contamination.

64. Atomiser cup condition & cleaning routine   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Burner cup not properly cleaned as needed (every start of shift).
   RECOMMENDATION: Clean the atomiser cup at the start of every shift and include it in the operator duties.

65. Atomiser V-belt (tension, alignment, wear)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Atomiser belt comes off at start-up: belt tension too high and motor/atomiser pulleys misaligned.
   RECOMMENDATION: Align motor and atomiser pulleys, set correct belt tension and keep a spare belt on site. Replace the belt if worn.

66. Burner quarl / refractory   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Burner quarl/refractory cracked or worn.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace burner quarl/refractory at the next service (refractory bricks and cement available from John Thompson).

67. Burner vanes, swirler & ducting   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Burner head air cone worn / vanes and ducting fouled.
   RECOMMENDATION: Clean and inspect burner vanes, swirler cup and ducting; replace worn components.

68. Primary air ducting & air leaks   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Primary air ducting leaking (temporarily sealed with tape); air leaks on ducting and smoke doors.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair all air leaks on ducting and smokebox doors to maintain proper combustion air control; replace ducting where required.

69. Air damper linkages & actuators   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Primary/secondary air damper linkages loose or movement restricted.
   RECOMMENDATION: Tighten and free up damper linkages so movement is clear and unrestricted; check actuator calibration.

70. Ignition assembly (transformer, probe, cable, pilot)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Ignition assembly damaged; gas pipe nozzle broken and temporarily repaired.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the damaged ignition components; reroute cabling to minimise damage and wear.

71. Flame scanner & controller   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Flame scanner failing / not detecting flame at low fire due to fuel quality, causing lockouts.
   RECOMMENDATION: Clean/replace the flame scanner, tidy and reroute cabling, and address fuel quality to ensure reliable flame detection at low fire.

72. Heat tracing on oil lines   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Heat tracing on burner oil lines damaged and not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the heat tracing on the burner and ring main oil lines and insulate the oil lines properly.

73. Minimum air pressure switch   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Minimum air pressure switch faulty / airline blocked.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the minimum air pressure switch and clean/reroute the airline. Burner ignition must be prevented when air pressure is not present.

74. Primary air differential pressure switch   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Primary air differential switch faulty / airlines blocked or damaged.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the differential air pressure switch; the burner must lock out if the primary air chamber differential is not balanced at ignition.

75. Burner door hinge limit switch   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Door hinge limit switch damaged; burner does not lock out when the door is opened.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace the door open limit switch so the burner locks out when the door is opened.

76. Ring main pressure stability   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: The ring main does not maintain constant pressure, negatively impacting combustion stability and burner efficiency.
   RECOMMENDATION: Resolve oil pressure fluctuations in the ring main. Install a separate pressure regulator for the burner and keep the ring main at a constant pressure so operators need not adjust supply pressure when switching between boilers.

77. O2 analyser & reference air pump   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: No O2 analyser installed / reference air pump and filter defective, affecting accuracy of oxygen readings.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install/replace the O2 analyser with a Lamtec LT2 KS1D-HT probe and control panel for improved combustion monitoring and control.

78. Combustion test (O2 across load range)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Flue gas oxygen content outside the optimal range.
   RECOMMENDATION: Reduce the flue gas oxygen content to between 3% and 6% by adjusting the fuel/air ratio. Yearly combustion set-ups should be done for optimal combustion efficiency.

79. Fuel flow meter   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: No fuel flow meter installed / HFO flow meter not working; fuel consumption not recorded.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install/replace the fuel flow meter to monitor and record fuel consumption.

--- Fuel Handling & Feeding — Biomass ---

80. Fuel storage / bunker condition & housekeeping   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Fuel storage area untidy and fuel exposed to rain, increasing the moisture content of the fuel.
   RECOMMENDATION: Store biomass fuel under cover and keep the storage area clean to control moisture content and fire risk.

81. Fuel moisture & quality as fired   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Fuel moisture content is high and the fuel contains contaminants (sand, stones, metal), reducing combustion efficiency and damaging the grate.
   RECOMMENDATION: Control fuel moisture (typically below 30-35% for grate firing) and screen out contaminants. Record fuel moisture daily.

82. Fuel feeding system (screw / ram / conveyor)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Fuel feeding system worn, blocking or feeding irregularly.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the fuel feeding system and set up feed-rate control linked to steam demand.

83. Metering bin / fuel level control   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Fuel level control in the metering bin is not working; fuel supply to the furnace is irregular.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the metering bin level sensors and feed control.

84. Burn-back / fire protection on fuel feed   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Burn-back protection not working or not installed, posing a fire risk in the fuel feed system.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install or repair burn-back protection (rotary valve or double flap, feed chute temperature sensor and water quench) and test it regularly.

--- Combustion — Biomass ---

85. Grate type & condition (moving / reciprocating / fixed)   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Grate bars worn, warped or missing, causing an uneven fuel bed and poor air distribution.
   RECOMMENDATION: Replace worn or damaged grate bars and restore correct grate movement and speed settings.

86. Clinker / slag formation on grate   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Clinker formation on the grate is restricting combustion air.
   RECOMMENDATION: Remove clinker; check the fuel ash content and furnace temperatures and adjust primary air and grate speed to prevent clinker forming.

87. Primary & secondary air distribution / dampers   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Primary and secondary air dampers are not working or not controlled, resulting in incomplete combustion.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the dampers and actuators and set up primary/secondary air distribution for complete combustion.

88. Furnace refractory & ignition arch   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Furnace refractory is cracked or damaged.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the damaged refractory.

89. Furnace pressure control   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Furnace pressure is not controlled, with a risk of smoke and flame blow-back.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the furnace pressure transmitter and ID fan control to maintain a slight negative furnace pressure.

90. FD / ID fans & VSDs   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: FD/ID fans are controlled by dampers only or the VSDs are faulty.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install or repair VSDs on the FD and ID fans for accurate combustion air control.

91. O2 analyser & combustion setting   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: No O2 analyser installed; combustion efficiency cannot be monitored.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install an O2 analyser and set combustion for the fuel fired (typically 6-8% O2 for biomass).

92. Unburnt fuel in ash   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: High unburnt fuel in the ash, indicating incomplete combustion.
   RECOMMENDATION: Adjust grate speed, fuel bed depth and air distribution to reduce unburnt fuel in the ash.

93. Furnace temperature probes   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Furnace temperature probes not installed or faulty.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install or replace the furnace temperature probes.

--- Ash Handling & Emissions — Biomass ---

94. Ash removal system (screw / conveyor / wet ash)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Ash removal system blocked, worn or leaking.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the ash removal system and schedule regular cleaning.

95. Grit arrestor / multi-cyclone   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Grit arrestor / multi-cyclone blocked or worn and the differential pressure is not monitored.
   RECOMMENDATION: Clean the grit arrestor / multi-cyclone, replace worn internals and install differential pressure gauges.

96. Spark arrestor / fire risk in flue path   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: No spark arrestor; sparks are carried over into the flue path.
   RECOMMENDATION: Install a spark arrestor upstream of any filter equipment to prevent fires.

97. Visible stack emissions   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Visible smoke at the stack, indicating incomplete combustion.
   RECOMMENDATION: Adjust combustion settings and complete emission testing.

98. Sootblowers / heating surface cleaning   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Heating surfaces fouled and sootblowers not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair the sootblowers and clean the heating surfaces regularly.

--- Steam System Profiling (report 4) ---

99. Steam distribution & operating pressures   [default priority: Medium]

--- Steam Traps (report 5.1) ---

100. Steam traps installed & trap set composition   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: The steam trap sets consist of only an isolation valve before the steam trap.
   RECOMMENDATION: Each trap set should consist of: two isolation valves (one on either side), strainer before the trap, steam trap, sight glass and check valve.

101. Traps passing steam?   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Most steam traps are functional but some are passing steam, seen from the feedwater storage tank overfilling and steam flow from the tank vent.
   RECOMMENDATION: Carry out an in-house audit to determine whether the correct traps are selected and sized; replace damaged traps; set up and maintain a steam trap database; test traps at least once a year and record all maintenance.

102. Condensate return pipework lagged?   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Condensate return pipework unlagged on some return lines.
   RECOMMENDATION: All condensate return pipework should be lagged and clad and returned to the feedwater storage tank. Install bypass valves to allow contaminated condensate to be dumped.

--- Water Treatment Plant (report 5.2 site) ---

103. Water softener working?   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Make-up water is not being softened as the unit is not working.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair, replace or have your local supplier investigate the water softener units and chemical dosing. Customer to supply service report.

104. Chemical dosing system   [default priority: High]
   FINDING: Chemical dosing pumps installed but chemicals are not being introduced into the feedwater storage tank.
   RECOMMENDATION: Restore chemical dosing. Regular inspection of the chemical drums is required and must be noted in the operator's logbook to prevent the boiler being operated without the required chemicals.

105. Water treatment specialist visits & reports   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Water treatment service reports need to be supplied for review.
   RECOMMENDATION: A local water treatment specialist must make regular visits to ensure operators and laboratory technicians maintain correct chemical dosing and boiler blowdown.

106. TDS / pH recording practice   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: TDS and pH are not measured and recorded daily.
   RECOMMENDATION: TDS and pH levels must be recorded for each shift in the daily logbook.

107. Chemical spillage / housekeeping   [default priority: Low]
   FINDING: Chemical spillage present on feedwater storage tank and pipework.
   RECOMMENDATION: Clean chemical spillage and repair the source; protect the tank and pipework from corrosion.

108. Feedwater tank & hotwell condition   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Hotwell tank and pipework leaking and heavily rusted / feedwater temperature too high due to condensate return.
   RECOMMENDATION: Repair/replace the hotwell tank and pipework; manage feedwater temperature to protect the feedwater pumps.

--- Insulation (report 5.3) ---

109. Steam pipework lagging (boiler house)   [default priority: Medium]
   FINDING: Steam pipework inside the boiler house is damaged or missing lagging.
